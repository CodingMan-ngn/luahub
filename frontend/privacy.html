Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

1. Information We Collect

When you log in with Discord, we collect your Discord User ID, Username, and Avatar. We do not collect your email or password. We also collect basic usage data (script hits, IP addresses for rate limiting) to keep the service running smoothly.

2. How We Use Information

We use your Discord data solely to create and manage your account, display your username on uploaded scripts, and show you on the active users list. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices, including encrypted databases (Supabase) and JWT-based authentication. However, no system is 100% secure. Do not upload personal information in your scripts or bio.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Discord for authentication and BoostyLink for monetization. By using LuaHub, you also agree to Discord's and BoostyLink's privacy policies.

5. Cookies

We use localStorage to store your authentication token. We do not use tracking cookies.
